Frequently asked questions

What date is 90 days from today?

See the table below the calculator; it updates every day. 90 days is about 3 months but not exactly, because months have 28 to 31 days.

How are months added at the end of a month?

If the target month is shorter, the date moves to that month's last day. Jan 31 + 1 month = Feb 28 (or Feb 29 in a leap year).

What counts as a business day?

Monday to Friday. Public holidays are not skipped, so adjust for holidays like Thanksgiving or Christmas yourself.
