Thirty days is a little over four weeks (4 weeks and 2 days). It is the classic length for a 30-day notice to a landlord, Net 30 invoice terms and 30-day return windows. Note that 30 days is not always the same as one calendar month.

Frequently asked questions

What date is 30 days from today?

It depends on today's date; the calculator at the top of this page shows it and updates every day. For example, 30 days from Friday, January 1, 2027 is Sunday, January 31, 2027.

How many weeks is 30 days?

30 days is 4 weeks and 2 days, so the end date falls 2 weekdays later than the start date.

Does 30 days include weekends?

Yes. 30 calendar days counts every day including weekends and holidays. If a deadline says business days, count Monday to Friday only; the business-day figure above shows how many weekdays are in the period.
