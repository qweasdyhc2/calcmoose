Percentage formulas

  • Percent change = (B − A) ÷ A × 100
  • A as a percent of B = A ÷ B × 100
  • A% of B = A × B ÷ 100
  • Sale price = price × (1 − percent ÷ 100); marked-up price = price × (1 + percent ÷ 100)

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate percent increase?

Percent change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100. Going from 80 to 100 is (100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 25% increase.

If something goes up 20% and then down 20%, is it back where it started?

No. 100 up 20% is 120; 120 down 20% is 96. The second percentage is taken from a bigger base.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

Percentage points are the plain difference between two percentages. A rate rising from 4% to 5% is up 1 percentage point, which is a 25% increase.
