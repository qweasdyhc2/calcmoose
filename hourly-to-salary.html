$Hourly to Salary Calculator

How the conversion works

  • Annual salary = hourly wage × hours per week × paid weeks per year
  • Monthly = annual ÷ 12, Biweekly = annual ÷ 26, Weekly = annual ÷ 52
  • Hourly from salary = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × paid weeks)

Biweekly and weekly amounts are based on the full year divided evenly, which is how most U.S. payroll systems spread a salary across pay periods.

For reference, the federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour (U.S. Department of Labor); many states and cities set higher minimums.

Where 2,080 hours comes from

A full-time U.S. job is usually 40 hours a week. A year has 52 weeks, so 40 × 52 = 2,080 paid hours. That number is the standard way to turn an hourly rate into an annual salary, and back:

  • Hourly to annual: hourly rate × 2,080. Example: $22.50 × 2,080 = $46,800.
  • Annual to hourly: annual salary ÷ 2,080. Example: $65,000 ÷ 2,080 = $31.25.
  • Quick estimate: double the hourly rate and add three zeros ($30 an hour ≈ $60,000). This uses 2,000 hours, so it is about 4% lower than the exact answer ($62,400).

Paid holidays and paid vacation are still part of the 2,080 hours, because you are paid for them. Only unpaid time off lowers your yearly pay, which is why the calculator lets you change the number of paid weeks. The U.S. Office of Personnel Management divides federal salaries by 2,087 hours instead, the average number of weekday hours in a year (OPM).

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert hourly pay to a yearly salary quickly?

Multiply the hourly rate by 2,080 (40 hours × 52 weeks). A quick mental shortcut is to double the hourly rate and add three zeros: $25 an hour is about $50,000 a year (exactly $52,000).

Why 2,080 hours?

A full-time schedule of 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours. The U.S. Office of Personnel Management uses 2,087 hours as the average for federal pay because some years have extra workdays; the difference is under 0.4%.

Does this include taxes?

No. These are gross (before-tax) figures. Federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare are taken out of your paycheck afterwards.

What if I take unpaid time off?

Lower the paid weeks per year. For example, two unpaid weeks means 50 paid weeks.

How much is $20 an hour a year?

At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, $20 an hour is $41,600 a year before taxes ($20 × 2,080). That is $3,466.67 a month or $1,600 every two weeks.

How much is $50,000 a year per hour?

$50,000 ÷ 2,080 hours = $24.04 an hour for a 40-hour week. At 37.5 hours a week (1,950 hours a year) it is $25.64 an hour.

How many work hours are in a month?

On a 40-hour week there are about 173.33 work hours in an average month (2,080 ÷ 12). Individual months have between 160 and 184 weekday hours depending on how many weekdays they contain.

How do I calculate yearly pay for part-time hours?

Multiply the hourly wage by the hours you actually work each week and then by 52. At $18 an hour for 25 hours a week, that is $18 × 25 × 52 = $23,400 a year.
