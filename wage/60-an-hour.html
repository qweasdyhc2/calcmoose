Is $60 an hour a good wage?

$60 an hour is 8.3 times the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and about 145% above the U.S. median wage for all workers, $24.51 an hour ($50,980 a year, Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2025). Whether it is enough depends heavily on where you live; rent and living costs differ widely between states and cities.

What about taxes?

The figures above are gross pay. Every paycheck has Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%) taken out, which is $9,547.20 a year on $124,800. Federal income tax, state income tax and any health insurance or retirement contributions come out on top of that, so take-home pay is usually noticeably lower.

Frequently asked questions

$60 an hour is how much a year?

$124,800 a year before taxes, based on a full-time schedule of 40 hours a week for 52 weeks (2,080 hours). With two weeks of unpaid time off (50 weeks) it is $120,000.

How much is $60 an hour a month?

About $10,400.00 a month before taxes (yearly pay divided by 12). Paid every two weeks, each paycheck is $4,800.00 gross.

How much is $60 an hour after taxes?

It depends on your state, filing status and deductions. Social Security and Medicare alone take 7.65% ($9,547.20 a year), and federal and state income tax come out on top of that.
