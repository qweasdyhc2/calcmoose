Weighted average formula

Course grade = Σ(score × weight) ÷ Σ(weight). Letter grades use the common 90/80/70/60 scale; your school may use plus/minus grades or different cutoffs. Want to know what you need on the exam? Try the final grade calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How is a weighted grade calculated?

Multiply each score by its weight, add the results, then divide by the total of the weights. Scores of 90% (weight 20), 80% (weight 30) and 70% (weight 50) give (90×20 + 80×30 + 70×50) ÷ 100 = 77%.

Do the weights have to add up to 100?

No. The calculator divides by the total weight you entered, so weights of 1, 2 and 2 work as well as 20, 40 and 40.

How do I find the score I need on the final?

Use the final grade calculator on this site. It solves for the exam score that gets you a target course grade.
