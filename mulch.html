Formula

  • Cubic feet = area (sq ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12
  • Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27
  • Bags = cubic feet ÷ bag size, rounded up

Frequently asked questions

How deep should mulch be?

2 to 3 inches is the usual recommendation for garden beds; 3 to 4 inches for paths. Keep mulch a few inches away from tree trunks and plant stems.

How much area does a cubic yard of mulch cover?

One cubic yard covers 324 square feet at 1 inch deep, 162 sq ft at 2 inches and 108 sq ft at 3 inches.

How many bags of mulch equal a yard?

Bagged mulch is usually sold in 2 cubic foot bags, so a cubic yard (27 ft³) is 13.5 bags. Some brands sell 1.5 or 3 ft³ bags.

How heavy is a cubic yard of gravel?

Roughly 1.3 to 1.5 tons, depending on the stone type and how wet it is. This calculator uses 1.4 tons per cubic yard as a planning estimate; ask your supplier for their figure.
