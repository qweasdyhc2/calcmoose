How the calculation works

Each time is converted to minutes after midnight. The difference, minus any unpaid break, is the time worked. If the end is earlier than the start, 24 hours are added, so 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM counts as 8 hours. The Dates & times tab counts across several days, and Add hours to a time answers questions like “what time is it 8 hours after 8:15 AM?”. For a whole week of shifts, use the time card calculator.

Reading shorthand like “8-430” or “11-6”

Work schedules are often written without AM/PM. “8-430” usually means 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM (8.5 hours), “830-430” means 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM (8 hours), and “11-6” means 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM (7 hours). Schedules in 24-hour (military) time skip AM/PM entirely: 14:30 to 19:00 is 4 hours 30 minutes, and 15:00 to 22:00 is 7 hours. If a range could cross midnight, such as 10-6 for a night shift, enter it as 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.

Some employers round clock times to the nearest 5 minutes, tenth of an hour or quarter hour. Under the U.S. Department of Labor rules, rounding is allowed only if it does not, over time, result in employees being paid for less time than they actually worked (29 CFR 785.48).

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate hours between two times?

Subtract the start time from the end time. From 9:15 AM to 5:45 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator assumes the shift ends the next day.

How many hours is 8 to 4:30?

From 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes, or 8.5 hours. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch it is 8 hours of paid work.

How many hours is 11 to 6?

From 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM is 7 hours. In 24-hour (military) time that is 11:00 to 18:00.

How do I convert hours and minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60 and add them to the hours. 8 hours 30 minutes = 8 + 30/60 = 8.5 hours. 7 hours 45 minutes = 7.75 hours. 7 hours 20 minutes = 7.33 hours.

How do I calculate hours between two dates?

Use the Dates & times tab. Enter the start date and time and the end date and time. The calculator counts every hour in between, for example Monday 9:00 AM to Wednesday 5:00 PM is 56 hours (2 days 8 hours).

How are unpaid breaks handled?

Enter the break length in minutes. It is subtracted from the total time between the two clock times. Paid breaks should not be entered, because they count as time worked.

How do I calculate a night shift that goes past midnight?

Enter the start and end times as usual. If the end time is earlier than the start time, 24 hours are added. 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 8 hours, and 7:00 PM to 7:00 AM is 12 hours.

What time is it 8 hours after 8:15 AM?

8 hours after 8:15 AM is 4:15 PM. Use the Add hours tab to add or subtract any number of hours and minutes from a time.
